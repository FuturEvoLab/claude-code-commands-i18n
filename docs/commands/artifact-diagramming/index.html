/artifact-diagramming

Skillv2.1.221+

Loads diagramming guidance for artifacts: when a diagram helps, what to draw, and SVG that stays legible in light and dark themes.

Best use

When you need a clear architecture diagram.

Related commands · Artifacts, slides & design

  • /artifact-capabilitiesLoads the reference for artifact runtime capabilities, such as calling connectors or offering downloads, and which your account has.
  • /artifactsLists artifacts you own or that are shared with you, then attaches one, opens it, or copies its link.
  • /datavizChart design guidance: picks the chart form, assigns color by role, and validates the palette for colorblind safety and contrast with a bundled script.
  • /designDrafts UI mockups, flows, landing pages or posters as artboards on one canvas (a Claude Design artifact); edit in a desktop browser and export PNG or PDF.
  • /design consentGrants or withdraws Claude's access to Claude Design.
  • /design-loginAuthorizes design-system access for /design-sync with your claude.ai account, when /design-sync or /design consent asks you to.
  • /design-syncConverts and uploads your repo's React design system to Claude Design so designs use your real components.
  • /slidesMakes a Claude Slides artifact from your brief.
