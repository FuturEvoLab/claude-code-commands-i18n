/privacy-settings

Views and updates privacy settings. Pro, Max and Ultra only.

Best use

To check how your data is used.

Related commands · Permissions & safety

  • /permissionsDecide what Claude may do without asking
  • /auto-mode-setupDrafts autoMode.environment entries from your project and recent sessions for you to review and save.
  • /fewer-permission-promptsScans transcripts for common read-only Bash and MCP calls and adds a prioritized allowlist to project .claude/settings.json.
  • /hooksViews hook configurations.
  • /sandboxToggles sandbox mode, on supported platforms only.
  • /update-configDescribe a settings change (allow a command, set an env var, add a hook) and Claude edits the matching settings.json.
