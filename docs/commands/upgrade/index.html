/upgrade

Opens the upgrade page in your browser.

Best use

When you keep hitting limits.

Related commands · Account & usage

  • /usageCheck cost and plan limits
  • /loginSigns in to your Anthropic account.
  • /logoutSigns out of your Anthropic account.
  • /passesShares a free week of Claude Code with friends; only shown to eligible accounts.
  • /rate-limit-optionsShows ways to keep working when a claude.ai limit blocks you: wait and auto-continue, add usage credits, or upgrade.
  • /statusOpens Settings on the Status tab: version, model, account, connectivity.
  • /usage-creditsConfigures or requests usage credits; Team and Enterprise members without billing access send a request to their admin.
