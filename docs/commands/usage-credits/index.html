/usage-credits

¥ May cost money

Configures or requests usage credits; Team and Enterprise members without billing access send a request to their admin. Previously /extra-usage.

Best use

Request credits from your admin when the team runs out.

Safety labels

¥ May cost money: Can consume paid usage or incur extra charges. Check /usage and the official pricing first.
